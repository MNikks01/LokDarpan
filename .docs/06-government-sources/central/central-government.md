## Union ministry / department inventory

The full IGOD Union Government crawl — **106 ministries and departments, 95 with an official website** — is machine-readable at [`.docs/06-government-sources/data/union-ministries.json`](../data/union-ministries.json), with each record carrying the organisation name, official URL, address, and the counts of sub-organisations (attached offices, statutory bodies, PSUs, schemes) that IGOD lists beneath it.

### Relevance assessment

§5 asks whether each ministry can contribute data. Ministries were assessed by mandate, not by inspecting each site:

| Relevance                          | Ministries / departments                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **CRITICAL**                       | Finance (Budget, Expenditure, CGA/PFMS); Road Transport & Highways; Rural Development (PMGSY/NRIDA); Panchayati Raj (LGD, eGramSwaraj); Statistics & PI; CAG (constitutional, not a ministry) |
| **HIGH**                           | Housing & Urban Affairs; Jal Shakti; Commerce & Industry (GeM); Electronics & IT (NIC, data.gov.in); NITI Aayog (NDAP, DMEO)                                                                  |
| **MEDIUM**                         | Education; Health & Family Welfare; Power; Railways; Water Resources — relevant from Phase 2 when the domain taxonomy widens (`.docs/15-scalability/scalability-plan.md`)                     |
| **LOW / NOT RELEVANT for Phase 1** | External Affairs; Defence (procurement is largely exempt/restricted); Home; Law & Justice; Culture; Parliamentary Affairs                                                                     |

**Caveat:** this is a mandate-based judgement. It has **not** been confirmed by inspecting each ministry's publication surface. A ministry marked LOW may still publish relevant works data.
