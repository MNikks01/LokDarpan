Not sent. When it is, record the date, the channel and any reference number in the registry; until
then the registry says `draft_ready` and nothing is collected from OMMAS.

## Before sending

1. **Confirm the addressee.** The legal notice at `pmgsy.dord.gov.in/Home/HomeLegalNotice/` names
   NRIDA as content owner. The office and address to write to are on the portal's own contact page;
   read it in a browser. Do not guess an officer.
2. **Consider an RTI in parallel.** It is a statutory route with a response timeline, and can ask
   narrowly whether a machine-readable export exists and on what terms.
3. **Do not overstate the project.** It is pre-launch, non-commercial and unfunded by any commercial
   interest; say so and nothing more.
