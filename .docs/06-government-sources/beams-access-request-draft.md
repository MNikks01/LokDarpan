Not sent. BEAMS is collected and used only to check other figures; nothing from it is displayed
until permission is recorded here and `PUBLISH_BEAMS_FIGURES` is set.

## Before sending

1. **Confirm the address.** The website policy says permission is taken "by sending a mail to us".
   The mail address is on `finance.maharashtra.gov.in`; read it in a browser. Do not guess one.
2. **Do not overstate the project.** Pre-launch, non-commercial, no commercial backing.

## Draft email

> **Subject:** Request for permission to reproduce BEAMS budget, release and expenditure figures
