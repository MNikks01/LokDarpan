Search finds verified figures by their evidence sentence and report pages by what they say, in
English and Devanagari, alongside places and report titles, from indexes rather than scans
(migration 0039). Place names match a substring or a near-miss spelling, in either script. Only
verified figures and republishable documents are searched; tender titles are not, while withheld.
